PlantVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

PlantVault helps you keep track of the plants and trees in your homes. This page explains what data the app handles, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

Data we collect

DataWhy
Email address (and your Google account's email, if you sign in with Google)To create and secure your account.
Homes, rooms, plants, trees, wishlist, watering and soil measurements, health-check resultsThis is the content of the app; it is stored so it syncs across your devices.
Photos you take or choose of your plantsStored with your plants, and sent for identification and health checks (see below).
The town or city you set for a home, and its coordinatesTo show local weather and adjust watering advice.
Number of AI requests per monthTo apply the usage limits of your plan.
Technical error reports (what failed, when, and your account ID)So we can find and fix problems. Never shown to other users.

We do not collect your device's GPS location, contacts, or advertising identifier, and we do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

ServiceWhat it receives
SupabaseHosts the database, photo storage and sign-in. Holds all the data listed above.
OpenAIThe photo and plant details you submit for identification or a health check, and species names for translation. Sent through our server, never linked to your email. Under OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train their models and is kept for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring.
Open-MeteoThe town name you search for and a home's coordinates, to return weather. No account information.
WikipediaSpecies names, to fetch the plant facts shown on the dashboard. No account information.
GoogleOnly if you choose "Continue with Google", to sign you in.

Plant facts and species care information are shared between all users, but they contain no personal data.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each account can only read its own homes, plants and photos.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the Dashboard, tap the account icon at the top right, then Delete account.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account, homes, rooms, plants, photos, measurements, health checks, wishlist and usage records. Error reports are kept but no longer linked to you. Nothing is kept in backups beyond the retention period of our hosting provider's daily backups (up to 7 days).

If you can no longer open the app, email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account within 30 days.

Children

PlantVault is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

If you live in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion by emailing CONTACT_EMAIL.
